REINFORCE algorithm

Initialize learning rate α
Initialize weights θ of a policy network πθ
for episode = 0,...,MAX_EPISODE do
    Sample a trajectory τ = s0, a0, r0, . . . , sT , aT , rT
    Set ∇θJ(πθ) = 0
    for t = 0, . . . , T do
        Rt(τ ) = P Tt0 =t γt0 −tr0t
        ∇θJ(πθ) = ∇θJ(πθ) + Rt(τ )∇θ log πθ(at | st)
    end for
    θ = θ + α∇θJ(πθ)
end for
